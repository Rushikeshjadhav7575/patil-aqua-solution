OUR STORY

A Local Idea With A Big Vision

PATIL AQUA SOLUTIONS is being developed as a custom water bottle manufacturing business from Jikthan, Chh. Sambhajinagar, Maharashtra.

The idea is simple: businesses already have their own identity, so why shouldn't their water bottles carry it too?

We aim to work with hotels, restaurants, companies, event organizers and other businesses that want customized bottles featuring their own brand name, logo and label.
